[Verse 1]
My sister left her songbook here
Her name in green across the front
She put a star beside the songs she loved
This one got three instead of one

I take my guitar off the wall
Slide the capo down two frets
That first line sounds different in this room
I like the way it settles in my chest

[Chorus]
Same song, in my key
Sounds a little more like me
Her voice takes the stairs by twos
Mine likes the landing and the view
Same words, room to breathe
I let the last note ring
Same song, in my key

[Verse 2]
She comes to take her songbook back
And hears me halfway down the hall
I hear her keys land in the dish
Then her high part through the wall

By the time she makes the doorway
We’re already on verse three
She pulls a chair up for the chorus
And leaves the melody to me

[Bridge]
When we were kids I used to mouth
The words a breath behind her
Now she waits to hear
Where I’m going with the line
I hold it half a beat
She grins and comes in right on time

[Final Chorus]
Same song, in my key
She takes the high part over me
Two notes hanging in the air
I never knew that chord was there
Same words, room to breathe
How good they sound with company
Same song, in my key

[Outro]
She takes her book and leaves the page
Three green stars above my chords
