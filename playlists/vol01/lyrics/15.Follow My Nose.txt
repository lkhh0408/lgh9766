[Verse 1]
One eye opens when your keys go click
You’ve got one shoe on, I’m already up
My tail taps twice on the umbrella stand
You take the hint and set down your cup

You check the weather, I check the breeze
Something good’s been underneath these trees
You’ve walked this block a thousand times
Today I’ll show you my side

[Chorus]
Follow my nose
That’s how the morning goes
Left where the rosemary grows
Then slow, slow, slow
You see a gate, I smell the sea
On somebody’s cuffs from half past three
Where have they been? Nobody knows
Come on, follow my nose

[Verse 2]
Outside the bakery you say my name
I know that voice, I know this game
I sit so straight the baker grins
And slips a biscuit in your hand

But there’s a boy with grass on his knees
And a tennis ball that smells like me
You ask him, “Where’d you find that thing?”
He points beneath the willow tree

[Short Refrain]
One good throw
Off I go
Straight back to you
With my morning news

[Bridge]
Back at the door, you crouch down low
There’s half the park between my toes
You brush me off, I lick your wrist
You smell like rosemary, too

[Final Chorus]
Follow my nose
That’s how the morning goes
Left where the rosemary grows
Then slow, slow, slow
You hang the leash beside the key
And break that biscuit just for me
I curl up warm across your toes
Dreaming where we’ll go

[Outro]
One ear up
One eye closed
