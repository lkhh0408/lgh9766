[Verse 1]
I pick a flat stone off the bank
It sits in the crook of my hand
One side’s warm from the sun
One side’s cool from the sand

I bend down low and let it slide
So close to the water it seems
For half a breath to be standing still
Then it skips out of reach

[Chorus]
One, two, three, still going
Four, five, would you look at that?
Six, and the water opens
Seven rings where the stone went flat
I like the part after letting go
When I’ve done all I can do
And it’s still going
Still going

[Verse 2]
I find a white one shaped like a dime
Turn it twice in my palm
It takes one hop, then settles in
And the circles travel on

They touch a leaf along the edge
It lifts, then settles too
I came down here to count the skips
Now I’m counting what they move

[Instrumental Break]

[Bridge]
I lie back on the wooden dock
One arm hanging off the side
A ripple taps my fingertips
I know that one was mine

[Final Chorus]
One, two, three, still going
I leave the numbers there
A reed leans into its reflection
And draws it back again
I like the part after letting go
When the lake takes up the tune
And it’s still going
Still going

[Outro]
Flat stone, open hand
One more from where I stand
