[Verse 1]
Six letters for a bird at sea
Your pencil drums against your knee
I say, “A puffin.” You count twice
It fits. We like that. Pass the ice.

Your coffee sweats a perfect O
Around a clue we almost know
We’ve got a booth, a pen that works
And Sunday in our hands

[Chorus]
Meet me on the other side of seven
I’ll go down and you go across
Somewhere in the middle of a word
Your train of thought runs into mine
You leave a letter there for me
I give you one you need
Meet me on the other side of seven
Where your answer crosses mine

[Verse 2]
You know the moons around a planet
I know the words to movie themes
You take the ones about the Romans
Leave all the leading ladies to me

Four down is asking for a singer
You hum two bars, I write her name
The waitress tops us up and whispers,
“My mother plays that song most days”

[Turnaround]
One square left
Under your thumb
Lift it up
We’ve got this one

[Bridge]
Of all the things I know about you
My favorite might be this
You do the whole thing in blue pencil
Then date it like a work of art
I used to toss the Sunday paper
Now there’s a stack beneath my bed
A hundred mornings in your handwriting
With half the words in mine

[Final Chorus]
Meet me on the other side of seven
I’ll go down and you go across
Somewhere in the middle of a word
Your train of thought runs into mine
You write the date beneath the grid
I draw a circle round it twice
Another Sunday for the stack
Where your answer crosses mine

[Outro]
You slide the paper over
I fold it on the dotted line
