[Verse 1]
On the way to the morning market
I practice asking for a pear
My little book falls open
To a picture of one right there

I know the word for yellow
I know the word for green
The woman in the apron
Holds one out for me

[Pre-Chorus]
She asks me, “For today?”
I close the book and say

[Chorus]
One for today, one for tomorrow
She picks them out, I watch her hands
One for today, one for tomorrow
She smiles at me, she understands
The words come off the page
And just like that, we’re talking

[Verse 2]
She taps the softer pear and says
“This one’s ready when you are”
We eat it leaning on the counter
With juice all down my arm

I tell her where I come from
She asks what grows back home
I draw a peach with my fingertip
She says the word real slow

[Instrumental Turn]

[Bridge]
All week I’d read both parts aloud
The shopkeeper, the guest
Turns out my favorite part is this
She gets to say what happens next

[Final Chorus]
One for today, one for tomorrow
She folds the paper round the green
One for today, one for tomorrow
Now I know just what she means
The words come off the page
She’s waving as I’m walking

[Outro]
A pear inside my pocket
A word for peach to keep
I say it all the way back home
It sounds a little more like me
