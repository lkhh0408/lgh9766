[Verse 1]
Milo drops a record in my crate
Says, “Side B, track three, just wait”
On the sleeve, three men in suits
One in a pair of silver boots

I’ve got a ten. It’s marked at five
The shop’s been open since a quarter to nine
He sets the needle in the groove
I’m halfway sold by bar two

[Chorus]
Spin it again
I want to hear how the bass comes in
Two beats late like it owns the place
Oh, spin it again
I came in here for something else
Now I want that sound all to myself
From the top, my friend
Spin it again

[Verse 2]
I take the sleeve up to the till
The woman there is smiling still
She taps the silver boots and says
“That’s my brother. He still has them”

I ask her, “Did you hear them play?”
She leans across the counter, says,
“Listen close when the band drops out
That laugh you hear? That’s me”

[Breakdown]
Two bars of keys
A tap on the rim
There’s her laugh
Then the bass comes in

[Bridge]
She writes the date across a paper bag
The name of the room where they cut the track
I know her laugh, I know his shoes
And I only walked in twenty minutes ago

[Final Chorus]
Spin it again
Now I know where the laugh comes in
One beat clear in the space they leave
Then the whole band leans back in
I came in here with a five to spend
Found a song with a story tucked in
From the top, my friend
Spin it again

[Outro]
Back at my place, the jacket hits the shelf
Side B, track three
I can find it myself
