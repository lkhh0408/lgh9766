Came out in tangerine
Caught myself in the bakery glass
Had to grin back at me

The woman folding up my jeans said,
“Got somewhere to be?”
I said, “Yeah, the corner store
For milk and something sweet”

[Chorus]

When a little kid two lanes across
Called out, “Hey, we’re dressed the same!”

He held his sleeve up next to mine
Two shades of tangerine
Then rolled a ball so slow we had time
To watch it hit all ten and scream
