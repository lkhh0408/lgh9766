[Verse 1]
I set my shoes up on the bank
And rolled my jeans up twice
Found a stone shaped like a dime
That fit my fingers just right
I turned my shoulder to the lake
And let that little silver dollar skate

[Chorus]
One, two, three, then I lost count
Little rings getting bigger now
One flat stone, one flick of the wrist
Ooh, that was a good skip
I came down here to cross the lake
On the little white ferry at half past eight
Now I'm on my knees picking out my next
Good skip

[Verse 2]
A man with a paper and a coffee cup
Folded the sports page, rolled his sleeves up
Said, “Mind if I try? It's been a while”
His first one flew about half a mile
He watched it go, then looked at his hand
Like he'd just turned ten again

[Chorus]
One, two, three, then we lost count
Little rings getting bigger now
Two grown kids on a strip of sand
With a whole flat beach in the palms of our hands
The ferry pulled in, the gate swung wide
He brushed the sand off his coffee lid
And tucked one stone in his coat for the ride
One more good skip

[Bridge]
Out on the deck with the wind in my shirt
I reached in my pocket and there was the first
Stone I'd picked up and decided to keep
Smooth as a coin from a country of feet
Dangling over the end of a dock

[Outro]
We stepped ashore on the other side
He caught my eye and stepped aside
I turned my wrist and let it fly
