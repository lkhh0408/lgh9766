[Verse 1]
Your postcard had a perfect view
Blue umbrellas, sea for miles
But halfway down the other side
You gave up talking about the island

“Three doors down from where I’m staying
There’s a bakery by the square
I bought a second bag of rolls
Just to stand a little longer there”

[Chorus]
Wish you could smell this
That’s all you wrote
A little cinnamon
On the envelope
I’ve seen a hundred pictures
Of that same strip of coast
But “wish you could smell this”
Is the one I like the most

[Verse 2]
I read it with the windows up
The grass next door was freshly cut
Somebody peeled an orange
On the step below my feet

I turned the postcard over twice
Then went upstairs to find a pen
Of all the things to tell you first
I knew just where I would begin

[Chorus]
Wish you could smell this
Fresh grass and orange peel
I’m sitting on our front steps
With the sunshine on my heels
You know the little patch of green
By the buckle in the street?
Wish you could smell this
From your bakery seat

[Bridge]
When you got back, you brought the recipe
Two aprons and a bag of flour
Soon the whole place smelled like your postcard
And the rolls still had a quarter hour

[Outro]
We sat there on the kitchen floor
Watching through the oven door
You leaned my way and whispered, “Well?”
I closed my eyes
“Yeah, that’s the smell”
