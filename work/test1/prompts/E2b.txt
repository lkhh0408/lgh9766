Continuous single-take aerial footage filmed from a flying camera above Daegu's 83 Tower (Woobang Tower) on a forested hill with amusement park rides, above a wide city. Point of view of the flying camera only; no aircraft is ever visible in the frame. The camera continues the same leftward orbit at the same speed, then gradually curves into a gentle forward flight toward the tower while slowly climbing, so the tower grows larger and the rides pass below the frame. As the sun rises higher, the warm golden sunrise light gradually and continuously turns into neutral clear morning daylight with a clean blue sky and white clouds. No cuts, no scene change, no zoom. Real camera movement with natural depth parallax. Buildings, the tower and roads stay rigid and keep their exact shape. Stable level horizon with no camera roll, smooth gentle motion.
