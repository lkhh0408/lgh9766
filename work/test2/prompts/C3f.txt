Continuous single-take aerial footage filmed from a flying camera very close to the observation deck of Daegu's 83 Tower (Woobang Tower), high above a wide dense city with mountains in the distance. Point of view of the flying camera only; no aircraft is ever visible in the frame. The camera is already moving when the shot begins and never stops. The camera slowly circles to the left around the observation deck at a constant gentle speed while rising slightly, keeping the deck near the center of the frame, so the city and the mountains behind it slide slowly to the right. The tower keeps exactly the same design throughout: a red and white antenna on top, a small cap, an upper observation ring, angled support struts and a large main observation deck with horizontal bands of windows. The tower is rigid and never changes shape or size except through perspective. No cuts, no scene change, no zoom. Real camera movement with natural depth parallax: the tower moves faster than the city, and distant mountains barely move. Buildings and roads stay rigid. The horizon stays perfectly level with no camera roll and no sudden moves; the speed stays constant and gentle for the whole shot. Clear bright daylight with a blue sky.
